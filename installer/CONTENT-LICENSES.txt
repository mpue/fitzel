Fitzel -- the content that comes with the installer
===================================================

Everything in this folder may be used for anything, commercial or not, without
asking and without credit. It is either public domain (CC0) or was made for
Fitzel.

CC0 1.0 Universal -- https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

  Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com)
    textures\*_diff*_4k.*, textures\*_nor_gl_4k.*   terrain, road and wall surfaces
    textures\cowboy_town_hall_4k.exr                 HDRI panorama
    models\boulder_01_4k.glb, models\Camera_01_4k.glb

  ambientCG (https://ambientcg.com)
    models\Bark001_2K-JPG_*, models\LeafSet024_1K-JPG_*

Made for Fitzel
    textures\road_puddles.png, textures\road_wet_grain.png
    (tiling noise for the road's wetness, generated by gen_wetmap.py in the
    Fitzel repository)

The *.meta file next to each asset is Fitzel's own record of it (the id that
projects refer to it by) and is part of the engine, not of the asset.

Your own textures, models and sounds go into textures\, models\ and sounds\
beside these; the editor picks them up when it starts.
